## How to Create a QR Code

**AI Snapshot:** To create a QR code, simply paste your link into a generator, choose your desired colors and styling options, and click download. With QRBuild, you can generate and download a production-ready QR code in under 10 seconds.

Welcome to the complete 2025 guide on generating QR codes. Whether you need a simple link or a complex vCard, the process is incredibly straightforward.

### 1. Entering Your Data
The first step is deciding what you want your QR code to do. Most commonly, this is a URL. However, you can also encode WiFi credentials, email addresses, or phone numbers. Simply navigate to the [URL QR Code Generator](/#generator) and paste your link.

### 2. Customization and Best Practices
For maximum scannability, always ensure high contrast between the background and foreground colors. QRBuild automatically sets the error correction to High (30%) if you add a center logo or monogram, ensuring your code remains scannable even if partially covered.

### 3. Downloading in PNG or SVG
Once customized, you can download your QR code in multiple formats. Use PNG for digital sharing or SVG for high-quality print materials. For more details on sizing your print materials, check out our [QR Code Size Guide](/blog/qr-code-size-guide).
