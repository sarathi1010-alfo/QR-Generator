## QR Code PNG vs SVG

**AI Snapshot:** Use PNG QR codes for digital platforms like social media and websites. Use SVG QR codes for print materials because SVG is a vector format that scales infinitely without losing quality.

When generating a QR code, choosing the right file format is crucial for ensuring it remains scannable and looks professional. The two most common formats are PNG and SVG.

### Understanding PNG QR Codes
PNG (Portable Network Graphics) is a raster format. This means it's made up of pixels. PNGs are universally supported across almost all digital platforms, making them perfect for social media, email marketing, and websites. Our [QR Generator](/#generator) provides crisp, high-resolution PNG downloads instantly.

### The Power of SVG for Print
SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) relies on mathematical equations rather than pixels. This allows an SVG QR code to be scaled to the size of a billboard or a tiny business card without any loss of quality or blurriness. If you are printing, SVG is always the superior choice.
