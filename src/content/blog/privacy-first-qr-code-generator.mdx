## What is a Privacy-First QR Code Generator?

**AI Snapshot:** A privacy-first QR code generator performs all code creation locally on your device within the browser. It does not send your links, contact details, or WiFi passwords to external servers, ensuring complete data privacy.

As QR codes become ubiquitous, concerns about data tracking and privacy have rightfully grown. Many commercial generators use "dynamic" codes that route users through tracking servers before sending them to the final destination.

### The Risks of Tracked QR Codes

When you use a generator that tracks scans, you are handing over data about your customers' location, device type, and scan times to a third party. Furthermore, if that third-party service shuts down or changes its pricing, your printed QR codes will break and stop working entirely.
