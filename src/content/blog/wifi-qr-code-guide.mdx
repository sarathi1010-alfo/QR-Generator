Tired of spelling out your complex WiFi password every time a friend or customer visits? A WiFi QR code is the perfect solution. It stores your network name (SSID), password, and security type in a format that smartphones can read and act upon instantly.

## Why Use a WiFi QR Code?

1.  **Convenience:** Users scan and tap "Connect". No typing required.
2.  **Accuracy:** Eliminates errors caused by confusing 'l' with '1' or 'O' with '0'.
3.  **Security:** You don't have to display your password in plain text on a wall.
4.  **Professionalism:** A branded WiFi sign looks much better in a cafe or office than a handwritten note.

## Is it Safe?
Yes. Creating a WiFi QR code is safe as long as you use a tool that generates the code locally in your browser. At **QRBuild**, we don't send your password to our servers. The "WIFI:" string is constructed right on your device.

## How to Generate One

### 1. Find Your Network Details
You need three pieces of information:
*   **SSID:** Your network's name.
*   **Password:** The key to join the network.
*   **Encryption Type:** Most modern networks use WPA or WPA2.

### 2. Use the QRBuild WiFi Generator
Navigate to our [WiFi QR Code Generator](/generator/wifi) and enter the details. You'll see the QR code update instantly.

### 3. Customize and Download
Choose a color that matches your decor. We recommend downloading the **PNG** for digital use or the **SVG** if you plan to print it on a large sign.

## Printing Tips
Place your WiFi QR code where it's easily accessible but not directly in the sun (to avoid fading). For a restaurant, table tents are ideal. For a home, a small framed print in the guest room or near the front door works perfectly.

## Compatibility
All modern iPhones (iOS 11+) and Android devices (Android 9+) have QR scanning built directly into their default camera apps. Users don't even need to download a special app!
